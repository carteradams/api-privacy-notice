Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026

This personal integration connects a single user’s own Whoop account to their assistant tools for personal use only.

Data accessed

With authorization, the app may read Whoop recovery, sleep, strain/cycles (including step count), workouts, profile, and body measurements via Whoop’s official API. It does not change Whoop data.

How data is used

Data is requested on demand to answer the user’s questions. Access tokens are stored encrypted by the self-hosted gateway. Whoop health data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train models beyond answering those requests.

Retention

Tokens are kept only while the integration is used and are deleted when the user disconnects or revokes access in Whoop settings. Health payloads are not stored as a durable archive.
